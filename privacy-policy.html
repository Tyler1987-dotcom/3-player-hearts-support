Privacy Policy

App: 3 Player Hearts for macOS

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how 3 Player Hearts handles information when you use the app.

Information Collection

3 Player Hearts is designed to run locally on your Mac. The app does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal information to the developer.

Local Storage

Game data and preferences, if saved by the app, are stored locally on your device. This information is not sent to the developer's servers.

Tracking and Third Parties

The app does not use third-party advertising or tracking services. It does not share your personal information with third parties.

Data Security and Retention

Locally stored data remains on your Mac under your device's control. You can remove locally stored app data by deleting it from the relevant app storage locations. Uninstalling the app may not automatically remove every associated data file.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or privacy practices change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
